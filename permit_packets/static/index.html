          Questions

          Is this stamped by an engineer?

          No. It's an automated drafting and NEC calculation tool, not a PE-stamped document. Some cities or jobs need a stamp or a licensed electrician's review; check with your building department.

          Who submits it to the city?

          You do. After you pay, your order page gives you the clean PDF, the individual files, the city's submission steps and copy-paste fields for the portal.

          Which cities does it cover?

          Any U.S. address works. Cities we've set up use their published checklist; everywhere else uses a general checklist, so confirm local requirements with your building department.

          Can a homeowner pull their own permit?

          Many cities allow owner-builder permits for a home you live in, and rules vary. Choose "Homeowner (Owner-Builder)" in the details step and the packet prints your name with no license number.

          How does payment work?

          You pay on Wave. Put your order code in the Wave note; once we see the payment your order unlocks. Bookmark your order page to come back to it.

          What if my city asks for changes?

          We can't guarantee approval. The packet follows the city's published checklist where we have it, and the $169 package adds a check against your city's requirements.

          Permit Packets

          Permit Packets is an automated drafting & NEC calculation tool. Documents are not PE-stamped and are not legal or engineering advice. Applicants are responsible for confirming their city's requirements and for submitting their own application through the city's portal.
