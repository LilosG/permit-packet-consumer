How credits work

  1. Buy a pack with the email you'll use for your jobs. We email you when it's ready.
  2. Preview your packet free as usual. At the package step, choose "I'm an electrician" or "I'm a contractor", then "Use a prepaid credit".
  3. Enter the email the credits were bought with. We email a confirmation link; opening it spends one credit and unlocks your packet.

Credits do not pay for the 24-Hour City-Checked Packet or the Panel Capacity Report. See pricing for the single-packet prices.
