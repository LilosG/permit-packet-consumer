Privacy

Permit Packets is provided by Lilos Growth. This page explains what we collect and who handles it.

What we collect

  • Your email address
  • Owner name, and contractor name and license number if you enter them
  • The property address, parcel and project details you enter or confirm
  • Order and payment status, and when an order was paid, released and downloaded

Card data is handled by Whop. We don't store it.

Why we collect it

To build your packet, deliver your order, and send you order emails (your order link, payment and release notices, and links you request with Find my order).

Service providers

We use these services to run the site. Each receives only what it needs to do its part.

  • Whop: takes payment and tells us when an order is paid.
  • Vercel: hosts the site.
  • Neon: hosts the database that stores orders.
  • Resend: sends order emails.
  • Address, parcel and aerial lookups: the address or map position you enter is sent to the US Census Bureau geocoder, FEMA/ORNL USA Structures building data, USGS National Map imagery, and county and city map services where we have them set up (for example San Diego County, Maricopa County, Pima County, King County, Mecklenburg County, the City of Las Vegas, Metro Nashville, San Francisco Planning and DataSF, NC OneMap and the Arizona Department of Water Resources). Address suggestions come from Mapbox when it is configured, and otherwise from Photon (OpenStreetMap). Mapbox satellite imagery is used when it is configured.
  • Google Analytics: measures how visitors use the site and how many orders result.
  • Google Fonts: your browser loads the site's fonts from Google.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site and to measure orders. It sets cookies and collects usage data such as pages visited and device type.

We don't sell your data

We don't sell your data.

Deletion requests

To ask us to delete your data, email hello@myelectricalpermit.com.
