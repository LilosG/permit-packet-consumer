What is Permit Packets?

Permit Packets is a product of Lilos Growth. You enter an address and a job, and the tool drafts the permit documents for four residential jobs: an EV charger, a panel upgrade, a subpanel and a hot tub or 240V circuit.

It is built for homeowners and for electricians and contractors who would rather not draft the paperwork by hand.

How are city checklists verified?

Each city we have set up is a jurisdiction module built from the city's own published checklist and checked against its official sources. Each module carries a last-verified date and a link to the city's page. A job a city has no checklist for uses a general NEC checklist, and the packet says so.

What is Permit Packets not?

  • It is not PE-stamped. Some cities or jobs need a stamp or a licensed electrician's review.
  • It is not legal or engineering advice.
  • It does not guarantee approval. The building department decides.
  • It does not submit anything for you. You confirm your city's requirements and submit your own application.

The Terms and the Refund Policy cover the rest.
